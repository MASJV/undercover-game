Party Word Game

Undercover

One phone. Everyone plays. Find the impostors — before they find you.

v2.1 · Works offline

How to Play

🎭 Roles

Civilian — Gets a secret word. Stay hidden, vote out impostors.

Impostor — Gets a similar but different word. Blend in!

Mr. White — Gets NO word. Listen, bluff, survive — then guess!

🔄 Each Round

1. Each player gives a 1–2 word clue about their word.

2. Discuss who seems suspicious.

3. Vote — most votes gets eliminated.

🏆 Winning

Civilians win if all impostors and Mr. White are eliminated.

Bad guys win if they equal or outnumber civilians.

Mr. White wins if eliminated but correctly guesses the civilian word.
